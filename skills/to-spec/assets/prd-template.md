# PRD Template

Prune sections that do not affect this decision.

## Outcome

Desired result, users or stakeholders, owner, and success evidence.

## Problem

Current behavior, observed pain, and why change is warranted.

## Scope

In scope, non-goals, and the smallest useful release.

## Requirements

Functional behavior, data, permissions, interfaces, and operational constraints.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Observable plain-bullet outcomes.
- Important negative cases and non-goals.
- Human approval or manual evidence only where genuinely required.

## Verification And Testing

Durable seams, commands, checks, readbacks, or manual evidence that prove the
criteria.

## System And Architecture Boundary

Include only when interfaces, integrations, ownership, data flow, or scale
affect the decision. Use a diagram only when it materially clarifies the
relationship.

## Risks And Dependencies

Dependencies, migration risk, security or privacy concerns, and mitigations.

## Rollout, Support, And Governance

Include only when release sequencing, adoption, operations, auditability,
support ownership, or decision authority matters.

## Assumptions And Open Questions

State each unresolved decision with its owner or resolution trigger.
