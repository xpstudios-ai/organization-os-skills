# Conditional Assessment Sections

Add a section only when its evidence and consequences affect the current
decision. Omit unused commercial and technical scaffolding. The SME core can
stand alone for a straightforward customer job.

| Trigger | Section and useful content |
| --- | --- |
| A buying question materially changes the decision or the user requests it | Buying decision: relevant decision maker, approval, alternatives and timing; no mandatory sales framework |
| Technical integration, data processing or material delivery risk is proposed | Feasibility: bounded current capability, missing data/rights, quality and validation, dependencies, security/access, integration, delivery ownership, human/system authority, acceptance and strongest supported claim |
| Value or a paid proposal changes the decision | Value/commercial basis: measurable baseline and outcome, costs/effort and assumptions, available budget evidence, pricing approval, contractual constraints and stop/go gate; unapproved prices remain internal proposals |
| A small test can resolve a material gap | Bounded test: question, permitted inputs, owner, scope/exclusions, measurable acceptance, review date/trigger, cost/time constraints, stop condition and subsequent decision |

For technical or operational work, include only constraints and evidence that
change this decision or were requested. A setup or connectivity check alone
does not prove delivery readiness.

For partner motions, assess the partner relationship separately from any named
customer opportunity. Do not infer customer need, rights, budget, sponsor or
success from the partner's access. Investor/advisor relationships without a
commercial problem use ordinary relationship records rather than buying fields.
