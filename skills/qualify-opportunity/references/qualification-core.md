# Qualification Core

Assess the decision and useful next step. Briefly consider four configurable
areas by default: **customer need**, **expected value**, **ability to deliver**,
and **buying decision/timing**. Use the workspace's names and depth when defined.
The user may select a subset or skip any area for this run. State the assessed
scope; excluded areas are **unassessed**, never positive findings. Missing
information stays unresolved. A name or introduction alone does not prove need.

| Area | Useful consideration |
| --- | --- |
| Customer need | Problem, desired result and who experiences it |
| Expected value | Why the result matters; evidence or assumptions about benefit |
| Ability to deliver | Relevant capability, constraints and what needs confirming |
| Buying decision/timing | Who can decide, next decision and timing when known |

Keep the result concise: recommendation and reason, supported facts, material
questions and next steps. GO / CONDITIONAL / NO-GO may be used for a matching
Qualification Verdict mapping. GO supports only the bounded next step assessed;
it does not establish an unassessed area's readiness. CONDITIONAL identifies
what needs confirmation; NO-GO identifies an evidenced disqualifier.

Use the existing questions table when it helps organize missing information.
Update rows in place rather than duplicating the register. Omit an unnecessary
or empty table; the existing six-column format is:

| Question | What we know | Information needed | Who will check | Why it matters | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Mark unconfirmed owners/dates honestly. Give only next steps that can change
the decision, with an owner or Unconfirmed and a known date or review trigger.
Cite accessible, audience-permitted sources for decisive facts. Distinguish
facts, requirements, assumptions and conflicts. Keep private capture, raw
transcripts, tool details and execution receipts out of the audience report.

## Approved Saves

Search and fetch the existing assessment before saving. Keep one assessment
child page under the Engagement's **Records** section and update it in place.
The child owns rationale, facts, questions and next steps. Do not copy a
qualification summary into the parent body.

An approved assessment save may also update matching **Qualification Verdict**
and **Assessment Date** fields when their identities and native mappings are
verified. Leave **Evidence State** and **Pursue** unchanged unless the user
explicitly instructs those writes. Leave any numeric Fit Score unchanged; no
scoring rubric or state conversion is defined. Read back the assessment and
any authorized property changes. If mapping, identity or access is missing,
return the draft and missing facts without guessing.
