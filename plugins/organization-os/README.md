# Organization OS

Use Meeting Runner to prepare a meeting or turn its notes into reviewed decisions and actions.

Connect Notion through your agent app, then give it the home and Meetings links for your copy. Ask it to confirm the account and workspace before saving. Review the draft, authorize the save, and check the returned page. This skills-only plugin does not connect accounts or start background jobs.

Keep the installed plugin managed by the app. Store your workspace choices separately. Receive approved releases through your installation channel; standalone ZIP imports require replacement.
